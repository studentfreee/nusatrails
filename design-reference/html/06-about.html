Our story

NusaTrails runs private trips across Java: Ijen, Bromo, Tumpak Sewu and Sukamade in East Java, plus Borobudur, Prambanan and the Jomblang and Pindul caves near Yogyakarta. We pick you up from Bali, Surabaya or Yogyakarta.

Why NusaTrails

Private small groups
Trips are for 2 to 4 people with your own car and driver. No big crowds, and the pace is yours.
Private English-speaking guide
Your guide walks every trail with you, explains the route and safety, and takes your photos.
Everything arranged
Transport, entrance fees, stay with breakfast, the Bromo jeep, Ijen equipment and water are included.
Pick-up from Bali, Surabaya or Yogyakarta
No need to plan the long drive to the mountains. We collect you from your hotel and drop you at the end.

Safety & responsible travel

Wild places have real rules.

Volcano hikes and wildlife visits come with real risks. This is how we look after you and the places we visit.

Ijen: a gas mask, a headlamp and the health certificate are included.
The blue fire descent is optional. If it is not for you, wait on the upper trail and go to the sunrise viewpoint.
Blue fire and sunrise depend on weather, safety conditions and official access, so we can never promise them.
Sukamade: follow the guide on light, noise, movement and photography around the turtles.
No alcohol during the trip. Travel insurance is your own responsibility, so please arrange it before you travel.

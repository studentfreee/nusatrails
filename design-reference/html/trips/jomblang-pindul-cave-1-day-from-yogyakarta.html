Itinerary

FULL DAYJomblang Cave, Pindul Cave & Oyo River Adventure

Get ready for an unforgettable adventure through the hidden natural wonders of Yogyakarta! This full-day experience combines the breathtaking underground beauty of Jomblang Cave, the unique river cave exploration of Pindul Cave, and an exciting tubing adventure along the Oyo River.

07:00 AM – Hotel Pickup in Yogyakarta

Your adventure begins with a convenient pickup from your hotel in Yogyakarta city, followed by a scenic drive to Jomblang Cave Basecamp.

Arrival at Jomblang Cave Basecamp

Upon arrival, you will receive a safety briefing and instructions from the local team. Get equipped with the necessary safety gear before preparing to descend into the spectacular underground world of Jomblang Cave.

Explore Jomblang Cave

Experience the thrill of descending into one of Indonesia’s most extraordinary vertical caves. Explore its ancient underground forest and witness the magical Light of Heaven, where sunlight streams through the cave opening, creating a breathtaking natural spectacle.

Return to the Surface & Lunch

After completing your cave exploration, you will be lifted back to the main ground. Take a break and enjoy lunch at the basecamp before continuing to your next destination.

Transfer to Pindul Cave Basecamp

Continue your journey to Pindul Cave, another remarkable natural attraction known for its underground river and unique cave formations.

Pindul Cave Tubing Adventure

After a safety briefing and equipment preparation, begin your tubing adventure through Pindul Cave. Float along the underground river on an inner tube while admiring the impressive stalactites, stalagmites, and fascinating rock formations inside the cave.

Oyo River Tubing Adventure

The adventure continues at Oyo River, where you will enjoy a scenic tubing experience surrounded by limestone cliffs and lush natural landscapes. Depending on the river conditions, you may also have the opportunity to experience small waterfalls and refreshing natural pools along the way.

Return to Basecamp

After completing both tubing adventures, return to the basecamp to freshen up and prepare for your journey back to the city.

Drive Back to Yogyakarta City

Relax during the return drive after a full day of exploration and adventure.

Drop-off in Yogyakarta City

Your trip concludes with a drop-off at your preferred location in Yogyakarta city, leaving you with unforgettable memories of the region’s incredible underground landscapes and natural beauty.

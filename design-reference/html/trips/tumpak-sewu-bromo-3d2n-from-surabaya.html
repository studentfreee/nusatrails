Itinerary

DAY 1Surabaya to Tumpak Sewu – Overnight Stay in Pronojiwo

Your Bromo and Tumpak Sewu Waterfall adventure begins at 09:00 AM. Our driver will pick you up from Juanda International Airport or your hotel in Surabaya before heading towards Pronojiwo, Lumajang, where your accommodation near Tumpak Sewu Waterfall is located.

The drive takes approximately 5–6 hours, depending on road and traffic conditions. During the journey, you will travel along toll roads and pass through rural villages, terraced rice fields, forested areas, and scenic cliffs.

Upon arrival at your accommodation, check in and take some time to relax after the journey. Enjoy the peaceful surroundings and get enough rest before the Tumpak Sewu trekking adventure the following morning.

DAY 2Tumpak Sewu Waterfall Exploration – Overnight Stay in the Bromo Area

After breakfast, we will begin the Tumpak Sewu excursion at approximately 07:00 AM. Our first stop will be the panoramic viewpoint, where you can admire the impressive waterfall cascading down the surrounding cliffs. Take this opportunity to capture stunning photographs of the landscape from above.

From the viewpoint, we will continue the trek towards the base of the waterfall. The walk takes approximately one hour and involves descending hillside paths before following the river through the surrounding terrain. Along the way, you will encounter several scenic spots, including Tetes Cave and Blue Lake, where you can stop to enjoy the natural beauty and take memorable pictures.

Once we reach Tumpak Sewu Waterfall, you will have time to appreciate its magnificent cascades and dramatic surroundings. Enjoy the scenery and capture photos of this remarkable natural attraction before we make our way back to the accommodation.

After the trek, we will return to the hotel, where you can freshen up and have lunch (at your own expense).

Later, we will depart for Sukapura, Probolinggo, where your accommodation in the Mount Bromo area is located. The journey takes approximately 3–4 hours, depending on traffic conditions.

Upon arrival, check in and enjoy the cool mountain atmosphere. Spend the rest of the day relaxing and preparing for the early-morning Mount Bromo sunrise tour.

DAY 3Mount Bromo Sunrise Adventure – Transfer to Surabaya

The final day begins at approximately 03:30 AM, when our 4WD jeep picks you up from your accommodation for the Mount Bromo sunrise excursion.

We will head to renowned viewpoints along the Tengger Caldera, including Penanjakan and Kingkong Hill, situated at an elevation of approximately 2,700 metres. From these locations, you can enjoy a beautiful sunrise accompanied by panoramic views of Mount Bromo, Mount Batok, and Mount Semeru.

After sunrise, we will continue across the expansive Sea of Sand surrounding Mount Bromo. From the jeep parking area, you can choose to walk or take an optional horse ride to reach the staircase leading to the crater rim.

At around 08:30 AM, we will return to the hotel for breakfast, a shower, packing, and check-out. Once everything is ready, we will begin the drive to Surabaya, which takes approximately 3–4 hours, depending on traffic conditions.

Upon arrival in Surabaya, your three-day adventure exploring Tumpak Sewu Waterfall and Mount Bromo comes to an end.

What to bring
Bromo peak temperature can reach 5-15 degree Celsius, the stronger the wind, the colder.
Prepare a pair of good walking shoes; trekking shoes, running shoes, hiking shoes (highly recommended for Tumpak Sewu climbing)

Raincoat and wet wipes for dust removal (can be purchased at petrol stations or convenience stores)

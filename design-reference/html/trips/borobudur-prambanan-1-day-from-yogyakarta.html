Itinerary

FULL DAYBorobudur & Prambanan Sunrise Private Tour
05:00 AM – Hotel Pick-up in Yogyakarta

Start your day with an early morning pick-up from your hotel in Yogyakarta. Meet your driver and begin a comfortable journey to explore two of Central Java’s most iconic cultural landmarks.

Transfer to Borobudur Temple

Enjoy the early morning drive through the peaceful landscapes of Central Java as you make your way to the magnificent Borobudur Temple.

Explore Borobudur Temple

Discover the beauty of Borobudur, a UNESCO World Heritage Site celebrated for its remarkable Buddhist architecture, intricate stone carvings, and rich cultural heritage. Enjoy time to explore the temple, admire its impressive structures, and capture memorable photographs.

Transfer to Prambanan Temple

Continue your journey to Prambanan Temple, enjoying a scenic drive to another of Indonesia’s most extraordinary historical landmarks.

Explore Prambanan Temple

Explore the magnificent Prambanan Temple complex, renowned for its towering Hindu temples and detailed stone reliefs depicting ancient Hindu epics. Take your time to appreciate the architecture, learn about its history, and capture beautiful photos of this remarkable heritage site.

Return Transfer to Yogyakarta

After visiting both temples, relax during the return journey to Yogyakarta.

Hotel Drop-off

Arrive back at your hotel, bringing your cultural adventure through Central Java to a comfortable close.

HIGHLIGHTSWhat makes it special
Magical Dawn Experience: Start your day in the early hours to witness a breathtaking sunrise over the Borobudur area as the morning mist clears.
Climb the Great Stupa: Ascend the ancient steps of Borobudur with special access to explore the upper terraces, intricate reliefs, and iconic stupas up close.
Explore Prambanan: Wander through the towering, intricately carved stone spires of Indonesia’s largest and most magnificent Hindu temple complex.
Flexible Booking Options: Choose our hassle-free "With Ticket" package or select the "No Ticket" option for maximum budget control and flexibility.
